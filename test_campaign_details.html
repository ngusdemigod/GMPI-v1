About this campaign

Expanding our sanctuary for the next generation

Our current sanctuary was built in 1989 and seats 1,200. Every Sunday, we turn away families because we simply don't have room. The House of Prayer Expansion will add 800 seats, a dedicated youth wing for 300 young people, and a state-of-the-art worship centre that will serve our community for decades to come.

Construction breaks ground January 2025, with a projected completion of September 2026. Throughout the build, all four Sunday services will continue uninterrupted in our current facility and in temporary structures on the new site.

Every dollar given goes directly to construction — no administration fees, no overhead deductions. Your seed today is a foundation stone tomorrow.
